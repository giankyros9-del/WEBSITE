About Me

Personal Background

I'm an Information Technology student fascinated by how technology works, from the hardware on the desk to the website on the screen. Hands-on practice and a habit of taking things apart to understand them have shaped my journey.

In Computer Fundamentals and Operations, I built a strong base in operating systems, file management, networking basics, troubleshooting, and productivity tools, and I started designing for the web with HTML and CSS.
